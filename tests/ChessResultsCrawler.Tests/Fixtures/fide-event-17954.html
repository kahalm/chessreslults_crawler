Event Name: I Cerrados Internacionales GM y MI "MyInvestor Casablanca".

Dates: September 5th to 12th, 2026. Venue: Stadium Casablanca, Vía Ibérica 69-77, Zaragoza, Spain.

Tournament System: Round Robin, 10 players per closed group.

Time Control: 90 minutes for the whole game, plus a 30-second increment per move.

Event Overview: The festival features Closed Tournaments designed for obtaining Grandmaster (GM) and International Master (IM) norms. The event is valid for FIDE ratings. Preference is given to active players holding international titles (IM, FM, WFM, WIM, or WGM).

Fair Play Measures: Strict FIDE anti-cheating guidelines for norm tournaments will be enforced. This includes a 15-minute delay in DGT board broadcasts, a complete ban on mobile phones, electronic devices, and all types of watches in the playing area.

Registration Fees: Fees vary based on the player's FIDE rating and the specific norm group (GM or IM).

Entry ranges from 200€ to 550€ for the GM Norm group, and from 200€ to 600€ for the IM Norm group.
